## Email 2 - Traditional or infrared

Subject: Traditional vs infrared: start with the room, not the marketing

The right choice depends on heat preference, available power, room and ventilation constraints, session style, and installed budget. Compare the practical tradeoffs in the [traditional vs infrared guide](https://backyardsaunapro.com/guides/infrared-vs-traditional-sauna/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=welcome_02).

Treat manufacturer benefit claims as claims. Product manuals, electrical requirements, and local installation rules are the decision inputs that must be verified.

## Email 3 - Planning and installation

Subject: The site details sellers need before quoting

Before requesting quotes, record the location, available dimensions, expected users, heater type, panel or chimney status, delivery access, target budget, and timeline. The free [sauna project planner](https://backyardsaunapro.com/sauna-planner/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=welcome_03) turns those details into a shareable brief without storing them on the site.

## Email 4 - Expensive mistakes

Subject: Five sauna costs buyers miss

Common omissions are freight and unloading, foundation or flooring, electrical or chimney work, permits, and weather protection. Use the [project cost calculator](https://backyardsaunapro.com/tools/sauna-cost-calculator/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=welcome_04) and keep product price separate from installed cost.

## Email 5 - Compare products responsibly

Subject: How to compare sauna offers without guessing
