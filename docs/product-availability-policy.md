# Product availability and imagery policy

Last reviewed: August 11, 2026

## Retail links

- Use an exact retailer product URL only while the listing is current, correctly matched, and reasonably available.
- Legacy sauna-cabin and portable-sauna Amazon ASINs route to a product-specific Amazon search. This avoids sending readers to one unavailable seller while preserving affiliate attribution.
- Button copy must say `Compare Current Amazon Listings` when the destination is a search page.
- Keep model specifications tied to a current manufacturer source. A research link may point to a backordered manufacturer page when the availability note is explicit, but it must not be presented as an in-stock purchase CTA.
- Do not imply that price, stock, seller, delivery date, or configuration is guaranteed.

## Images

- Exact product images must be owned, licensed, supplied by an approved partner, or delivered through an affiliate program's approved product-content tools.
- Do not copy Amazon or manufacturer product images manually.
- Original editorial images may illustrate a product category. When placed beside a named model, label them `Representative setup; not the exact model.`
- Preserve the documented Sun Home content-library images and protected placements.

## Review cadence

- Recheck high-traffic commercial pages weekly.
- Recheck lower-traffic product pages monthly.
- When an exact listing is unavailable, replace it with a product-specific search or a verified in-stock retail partner. Do not substitute an unrelated model under the old product name.
