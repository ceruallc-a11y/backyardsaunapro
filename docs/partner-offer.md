# Backyard Sauna Pro Partner Offer

## Positioning

Backyard Sauna Pro helps homeowners compare sauna types, estimate project requirements, and prepare for installation. Partnerships must improve a buyer's decision rather than disguise advertising as independent editorial coverage.

## Audience

- Homeowners researching indoor and outdoor saunas
- Buyers comparing traditional, infrared, and wood-fired options
- Buyers planning electrical, foundation, delivery, and installation work
- Sauna owners shopping for heaters, accessories, and maintenance products

Verified audience size, geography, device mix, subscriber count, and conversion metrics must be inserted from current analytics before this offer is sent externally.

## Available formats

- Clearly labeled sponsored buyer-guide placement
- Newsletter sponsorship after the list and engagement are validated
- Product-launch feature based on documented product information
- Showroom, installer, or completed-project case study
- Sponsored directory listing after traffic and lead quality are validated
- Brand interview or product-data collaboration
- Custom research sponsorship
- Content licensing or original photography/video collaboration when original assets exist

## Editorial rules

- Payment never permits fabricated testing, experience, performance, or customer claims.
- Sponsored relationships are disclosed near the affected recommendation.
- Product facts are attributed to the manufacturer or another named source.
- Pricing is described as current at review time and subject to change.
- The publication may correct or update inaccurate information.
- Medical claims are not accepted.
- Existing contractual placements are not displaced without owner approval.

## Proof available today

- A prior paid sauna-brand collaboration exists and may be referenced only in terms approved by the owner and the partner agreement.
- The site contains more than 100 sauna-focused pages and current affiliate integrations.
- All traffic, subscriber, click, lead, and revenue figures remain placeholders until verified.

## Inquiry path

Prospective partners can email `support@backyardsaunapro.com` with:

- Company and product category
- Requested placement or collaboration
- Target timing
- Budget range
- Affiliate or retailer program details
- Product data and approved asset availability

No placement is accepted automatically. Every proposal receives editorial, compliance, audience-fit, and conflict review.
