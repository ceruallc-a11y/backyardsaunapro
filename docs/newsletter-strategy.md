# Newsletter Strategy

## Promise

Practical sauna buying, planning, installation, ownership, and maintenance guidance for homeowners. The newsletter should help a reader make one clearer decision, not merely announce new articles.

## Segmentation question

Ask new subscribers which stage best fits them:

- Comparing sauna types
- Planning space, power, and budget
- Ready to buy within 90 days
- Already own a sauna
- Industry professional

Store the answer as a subscriber field or tag and use it to choose later recommendations.

## Signup placements

- Homepage after the first decision paths
- Article footer
- Relevant commercial pages after comparison content
- Dedicated `/newsletter` page
- Lead-form confirmation, with separate consent
- A non-intrusive scroll prompt only after baseline conversion is measured

## Welcome sequence outline

1. Welcome, set expectations, and ask buyer stage.
2. Traditional versus infrared decision guide.
3. Planning checklist covering space, power, foundation, delivery, and permits.
4. Expensive buying mistakes and how to avoid them.
5. Stage-relevant product, retailer, or installer paths with disclosures.
6. Invitation to submit a sauna-planning request.

## Operating rules

- Do not send until sender name, reply-to address, platform, content, cadence, and legal footer are owner-approved.
- Use confirmed opt-in when practical and preserve consent records.
- Never add lead-form contacts to marketing without separate consent.
- Label sponsored content and affiliate relationships clearly.
- Remove stale deals and avoid guaranteed-price language.
- Start at no more than one useful issue per week.

## Measurement

Track signup source, subscriber growth, delivery, unique open rate as directional only, click rate, unsubscribe rate, complaint rate, affiliate clicks, planning requests, and revenue by campaign. UTM-tag every site link.
